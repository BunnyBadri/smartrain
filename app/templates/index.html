Soft Computing Technique Justification (Viva Guide)

Role of ANN: Meteorological parameters (pressure drops, moisture saturation, thermal convective currents) exhibit complex nonlinear physical interactions that cannot be accurately captured by simple linear regressors. The MLP learns these multidimensional nonlinear patterns directly from historical observations.

Role of Fuzzy Logic: Traditional ML models function as unexplainable numerical "black boxes". A farmer or reservoir controller cannot gauge operational risk from a bare number alone. The Mamdani FIS provides human-centered, linguistically transparent decision support.
